# OmniMind — Approved Roadmap

The approved order must not be changed without discussion:
1. **Foundation & Architecture** — identity, architecture, technology, repository structure, database, accounts and security.
2. **OmniMind Workspace** — Command Center, navigation, workspaces, adaptive UI, settings, themes and responsive layouts.
3. **Omni Tasks** — tasks, priorities, dates, recurrence, subtasks, goals, Eisenhower matrix and categories.
4. **Omni Planning & Calendar** — day/week/month/year and custom periods; three detail levels; time allocation and rescheduling.
5. **Omni Notes & Documents** — editor, headings, lists, tables, folders, links, search, autosave and attachments.
6. **Omni Knowledge** — linked concepts and notes, suggested links, graph view and knowledge search.
7. **Omni Study** — flashcards, note-to-card conversion, spaced repetition and review sessions.
8. **Omni Focus** — timer, Pomodoro, task-linked time, work hours, breaks and reports.
9. **Omni AI** — central assistant, natural-language task capture, goal decomposition, planning, knowledge, study and performance; specialized assistants later.
10. **Integration & Optimization** — module integration, sync, offline support, performance, battery, backup, security, testing and launch.

Planning supports custom date ranges and three user-selected detail levels: comprehensive, balanced and lightweight. AI-proposed changes require explicit user approval.
